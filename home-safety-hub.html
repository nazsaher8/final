Home should be a place where you can feel secure, respected and comfortable. Learn about healthy boundaries, trusted support, privacy and practical safety planning for situations at home.

A Safer Home Begins With Awareness

Safety at home can involve physical surroundings, digital privacy, personal boundaries and knowing who you can contact when you need support. A safety plan should be practical and appropriate for your own circumstances.

Explore Home Safety

Build awareness around different parts of home safety.

🚪

Personal Boundaries

Everyone has the right to communicate personal boundaries and expect respectful behavior from others.

🔐

Privacy

Think about who has access to your personal belongings, accounts, devices and private information.

📞

Trusted Contacts

Keep contact information for people you trust accessible when you need advice, assistance or emotional support.

🧭

Safety Planning

Think ahead about where you could go and who you could contact if you ever needed additional support.

Build a Personal Safety Plan

A safety plan is not about expecting something bad to happen. It is simply a way to think ahead and identify useful options.

Healthy Boundaries at Home

Respect and consent matter in every environment.

💬 Communication

Healthy communication allows people to express discomfort, preferences and boundaries without being pressured or mocked.

🔒 Privacy

Personal spaces, devices, accounts and belongings should be treated with appropriate respect.

✋ Saying No

A person's boundaries deserve to be taken seriously. Pressure or unwanted behavior should not be treated as normal or acceptable.

🤝 Getting Support

Speaking with a trusted person or appropriate support service can help when a situation becomes difficult or unsafe.
