🛡️ URGENT SAFETY

If You Feel Unsafe, Get Support.

If you are in immediate danger, focus on reaching a safer place and contacting the appropriate emergency service or trusted person in your area.

Get Support → Contact HerShield
🛡️

What Can You Do Right Now?

Focus on immediate safety and choose the action that fits your situation.

🏃

Move to Safety

If possible, move toward a safer location, a populated area, or somewhere you feel secure.

📞

Contact Emergency Services

If you are facing immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service available in your location.

🤝

Reach a Trusted Person

Contact someone you trust and tell them what is happening when it is safe to do so.

📱

Keep Your Phone Available

If safe, keep your phone accessible so you can communicate with someone who can help.

🔐

Protect Your Privacy

Avoid sharing sensitive information publicly and use trusted communication channels.

🧭

Choose Your Next Step

Once you are safer, explore the appropriate HerShield hub for additional information.

Remember Three Simple Steps

Keep your focus on immediate safety first.

1

Stay Aware

Pay attention to your surroundings and identify a safer place or person you can reach.

2

Reach Out

Contact emergency services or someone you trust when you can do so safely.

3

Get Support

When immediate danger has passed, explore appropriate support and reporting options.

Important Safety Note

HerShield is an educational awareness project. It is not an emergency service and does not replace emergency responders, medical care, counseling, or professional legal advice. For immediate danger, use the appropriate emergency service available in your location.
