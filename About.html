🛡️ ABOUT HERSHIELD

Your Safety.
Your Voice.
Your Shield.

HerShield is a safety-awareness platform designed to help girls and women understand safety, recognize concerning situations, explore support options, and make informed choices about their next steps.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

HerShield was created to make safety information easier to understand, easier to access, and easier to act upon.

🎯

Our Purpose

The purpose of HerShield is to provide a clear and organized digital space where users can learn about different types of harassment and safety concerns, and explore practical support and reporting information.

💙

Our Vision

Our vision is to encourage safer environments where people understand boundaries, respect, privacy, digital safety, reporting choices, and the importance of reaching out for support.

WHY HERSHIELD?

Knowledge Can Help People Feel Prepared

Safety information can sometimes feel confusing or difficult to find. HerShield brings different safety topics together in one organized platform.

Instead of focusing on only one environment, HerShield provides different hubs for situations that may occur in everyday life.

  • ✓ Easy-to-understand safety awareness
  • ✓ Practical safety planning information
  • ✓ Reporting and documentation guidance
  • ✓ Support-focused resources
  • ✓ Digital and personal safety awareness
7 Hubs Different environments and safety areas
Safety First Information designed around awareness and preparedness
One Platform A central place to explore safety and support information
