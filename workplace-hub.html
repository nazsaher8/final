WORKPLACE AWARENESS

A respectful workplace starts with awareness.

Professional environments involve people with different roles, responsibilities and levels of authority. Understanding appropriate boundaries can help create a respectful workplace and help people recognize when behavior may need to be addressed through appropriate channels.

PROFESSIONAL PRINCIPLES

Four foundations of workplace safety.

Awareness can help people understand healthy professional environments.

🤝

Respect

Professional communication should maintain dignity and respect.

🚧

Boundaries

Clear personal and professional boundaries are important at work.

⚖️

Fairness

Workplace concerns should be handled through appropriate processes.

🛡️

Support

Employees should know where appropriate support and reporting channels exist.

UNDERSTANDING BEHAVIOR

What should you recognize?

🟢 Professional Environment

Examples of respectful workplace practices can include:

  • Respectful communication
  • Appropriate professional boundaries
  • Clear workplace expectations
  • Professional treatment of colleagues
  • Accessible workplace policies

🟠 Concerning Behavior

Situations that may warrant attention can include:

  • Repeated unwanted communication
  • Unwanted physical contact
  • Threatening or intimidating behavior
  • Repeatedly ignoring boundaries
  • Inappropriate use of authority
