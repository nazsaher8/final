CAMPUS AWARENESS

Every student deserves a safe learning environment.

Schools and colleges should provide environments where students can learn without fear or unwanted behavior. HerShield provides educational information to help students recognize concerning situations and understand where they may seek help.

SAFETY AWARENESS

Recognize concerning situations.

🛡️ Healthy Boundaries

  • Respect for personal space
  • Communication without intimidation
  • Freedom to say no
  • Respectful interactions
  • Appropriate professional behavior

⚠️ Concerning Behavior

  • Repeated unwanted communication
  • Threatening or intimidating behavior
  • Unwanted physical contact
  • Pressure to keep inappropriate behavior secret
  • Repeatedly ignoring clearly expressed boundaries
NEED SUPPORT?

Choose what you need.

HerShield is an educational platform. For an actual incident, consider contacting a trusted person, your institution's appropriate reporting channel, or local emergency services if you are in immediate danger.
