Safety Belongs Everywhere

Harassment and uncomfortable situations can occur in many public environments. Awareness, preparation, clear boundaries and knowing where to seek help can make it easier to respond to concerning situations.

Create Your Safety Plan

A simple plan can help you think ahead before travelling, attending an event or visiting an unfamiliar public place.

  • ✓ Know where you are going
  • ✓ Keep a trusted person informed when appropriate
  • ✓ Identify places where you can ask for help
  • ✓ Keep important contacts accessible
  • ✓ Trust your boundaries and seek support if needed
